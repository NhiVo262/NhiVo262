## About Me

I'm a **Robotics R&D Specialist at Eastern International University (EIU), Vietnam**, with a background in Control and Automation Engineering from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT).

My work focuses on **robotics software development, autonomous systems, and multi-robot coordination**, particularly in ROS 2-based systems, Open-RMF, and PX4-based UAV platforms.

I am interested in building modular robotics software, integrating distributed robotic systems, and evaluating their performance through simulation and real-world experimentation.

Beyond engineering, I am also a **STEM educator and robotics mentor at EIU FabLab**, supporting students in developing practical engineering skill.

## Selected Engineering Projects

### 01. Open-RMF Multi-Fleet Benchmarking

**Performance Evaluation Framework for Multi-Robot Fleet Coordination**

Developing a benchmarking framework to systematically evaluate the performance and efficiency of multi-fleet coordination using Open-RMF.

**Key Contributions**
- Design and implement performance metrics for task execution, scheduling efficiency, robot utilization, and traffic management.
- Develop tools for automated data collection, performance monitoring, and benchmarking analysis.
- Integrate Zenoh as communication middleware for distributed robotic systems.
- Design simulation-based evaluation scenarios involving multiple robots and heterogeneous fleets.
- Analyze coordination performance, system scalability, and resource utilization under different operating conditions.

**Technologies:** `ROS 2` `Open-RMF` `C++` `Python` `Zenoh` `Gazebo`

**Focus:** Multi-Fleet Coordination · Performance Evaluation · Benchmarking · Distributed Robotics

### 02. PX4-Based Autonomous UAV System

**ROS 2-Integrated Autonomous Quadcopter Platform**

Developing an autonomous UAV platform integrating PX4 flight control with ROS 2-based onboard computing and perception.

**Key Contributions**

- Develop offboard control and autonomous flight functionalities.
- Integrate LiDAR, camera, and onboard sensors for environmental perception.
- Implement and evaluate obstacle avoidance approaches.
- Validate autonomous flight applications using Gazebo simulation and hardware testing.

**Technologies:** `PX4` `ROS 2` `Python` `Gazebo` `LiDAR` `Computer Vision`
