### About Me

I'm a **Robotics R&D Specialist at Eastern International University (EIU)**, Vietnam, with a background in **Control and Automation Engineering** from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT).

My work focuses on developing and integrating **ROS 2-based robotic systems**, with interests in autonomous navigation, distributed robotics, multi-robot coordination, and PX4-based UAVs.

I also teach STEM and mentor robotics students at **EIU FabLab**.
